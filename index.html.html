Paw Pow! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Paw Pow! ("the game") is made by Nekomama Studio, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email us at stomperr7683@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we don't run servers. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Our ad partners collect some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • You choose whether ads can be personalized, and you can change that any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • Players under 13 only get non-personalized ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in a file on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: coins, high score, play statistics, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, daily gift streak.
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration.
  • Your privacy choices (personalized ads, "Do Not Sell or Share") and the birth year you enter. The birth year is only used on your device to choose the right ad settings.

Uninstalling the game deletes this information.

Advertising

The game uses LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation service) to show ads from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, both run by Unity Technologies. To show ads, measure them, cap how often you see them, and prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), when allowed.
  • Your IP address and an approximate location based on it (country or city, not your exact location).
  • Device information, such as model, operating system, language, screen size and network type.
  • How you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and installs.

If you allow personalized ads, partners may also use this information to show ads based on your interests. If you don't, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Unity explains how it handles this information in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: the game asks on first launch. Change it any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Info: turn it on in Settings > Privacy. We don't sell your information for money. Some U.S. state laws (such as California's) may consider sharing information with ad partners a "sale" or "sharing", and this setting opts you out.
  • Device settings: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy settings. On iPhone and iPad, you can control tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children

Paw Pow! is made for a general audience. On first launch, the game asks for the player's birth year without suggesting an answer. If the player is under 13, the game tells our ad partners to treat them as a child under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). That player only sees non-personalized ads, their information isn't used for interest-based advertising, and they are not asked about personalized ads.

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child's information was collected, email us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or U.S. states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. We don't hold any of your information ourselves, since everything the game saves stays on your device. For information held by our ad partners, use the choices above or contact Unity through its privacy policy. You can also email us and we'll help.

Security and retention

Information the game saves stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Our ad partners keep the information they collect according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
